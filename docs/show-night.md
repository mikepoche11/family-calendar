# Show night

The Marbles page shows whose night it is to pick the TV show, with that kid's avatar and colour and a little TV
that changes with the theme (10 versions, one per theme, all original art).

- **Turns:** Aaron and Jordyn take turns, starting with Aaron on Sunday, November 22, 2026. It moves to the next kid
  every night at midnight (Central time) by calendar date, so it stays right even if the screen was off.
- **Swap:** tap **Swap** if a night gets skipped or they trade. Tonight goes to the other kid and the turns carry on
  from there. Tap it again to put it back.
- **Who takes turns:** **Jars & rewards** → *Show night*. Tap kids in the order they take turns (the number on each
  chip is their place). Whoever picks tonight keeps tonight when you change the list.

The TV drawings live in `tools/holiday-icons/build.py` (the `TV` section); rerun the script to regenerate
`frontend/src/holidayArt.js`.
